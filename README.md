## Overview

Support Intelligence is an internal analytics and automation platform that transforms raw customer support data into actionable insights. Instead of manually reviewing tickets, the platform automatically categorizes, analyzes, and generates AI-powered executive reports — allowing the support team to focus on resolution rather than reporting.

---

## Features

- **Ticket Analytics Dashboard** — Week-over-week delta tracking, volume trends, and category breakdowns
- **AI Ticket Categorization** — Rule-based + Gemini AI hybrid classification of incoming support tickets
- **Automated Refund Workflow** — Chargebee Data Connector integration with eligibility logic and audit trail
- **Executive Report Generator** — AI-generated weekly/monthly summaries with key insights and recommendations
- **Billing Dispute Management** — Centralized view of payment-related escalations pulled directly from Chargebee
- **GDPR Compliance Tracker** — Monitors and flags data-related requests and their resolution status

---

## AI Automation

The platform uses a two-layer categorization approach:

1. **Rule-based layer** — Fast pattern matching for known ticket types (refunds, login issues, billing questions)
2. **Gemini AI layer** — Handles ambiguous tickets, generates category confidence scores, and drafts suggested responses

Weekly executive reports are fully generated by Gemini based on aggregated ticket data, including anomaly detection and recommended actions.
